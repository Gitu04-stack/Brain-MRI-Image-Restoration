Key Architectural Innovations:
  • Global Residual Learning: The network predicts the residual noise/artifact map $R(x)$, adding it to the input slice: $I_{\text{restored}} = \text{clamp}(I_{\text{degraded}} + R(x), 0.0, 1.0)$.
  • Multi-Scale Skip Connections: Preserves low-level anatomical edges and high-frequency boundary textures across contracting and expanding paths.
  • Edge-Preserving Charbonnier Loss: Prevents the over-smoothing typical of Mean Squared Error by using $\sqrt{(y - \hat{y})^2 + \epsilon^2}$.
