Mathematical Formulations & Clinical Interpretation
1. Mean Squared Error (MSE)

Computes the average squared pixel-wise intensity difference across the $M \times N$ matrix:

MSE = (1 / MN) Σ [I_clean(x,y) - I_restored(x,y)]²
Provides a direct measure of residual noise energy, but does not capture human perceptual or anatomical structural correlations.
2. Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio (PSNR)

Logarithmic decibel (dB) ratio between maximum possible image signal intensity and the corrupting MSE:

PSNR = 10 · log₁₀ (MAX² / MSE)
In normalized [0.0, 1.0] floating-point MRI slices, $MAX = 1.0$. Standard clinical benchmarks consider $\ge 20 \text{ dB}$ acceptable restoration under multi-degradation.
3. Structural Similarity Index (SSIM)

Perception-based metric evaluating luminance ($l$), contrast ($c$), and structural correlation ($s$):

SSIM(x,y) = [l(x,y)]α · [c(x,y)]β · [s(x,y)]γ
Crucial for Brain MRI diagnostic fidelity, ensuring tumor boundaries, gray/white matter differentiation, and ventrical margins are structurally preserved.
