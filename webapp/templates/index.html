Comparative Analysis of Brain MRI Image Restoration Techniques Under Different Degradation Conditions

This study investigates the mathematical modeling of physical and acquisition artifacts in clinical T1-weighted Brain MRI scans—including complex Rician noise, additive Gaussian perturbations, linear motion blur, and spatial low-resolution subsampling—and evaluates traditional spatial/statistical restoration techniques against deep residual U-Net architectures.
